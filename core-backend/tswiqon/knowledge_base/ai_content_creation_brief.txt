Example Brief for an AI Content Creation Company:

Company Specialization: AI-driven blog post and social media content generation.
Target Audience: Small to medium-sized businesses (SMBs) looking for affordable, high-quality content.
Key Services:
- Blog post writing (various lengths and topics).
- Social media updates (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook).
- Content idea generation.
- Basic SEO optimization of content.
Value Proposition: Fast, scalable, and cost-effective content creation to boost online presence.
Core AI Roles to Consider:
- Chief Content Strategist AI (oversees content plans, quality).
- AI Writing Agent (specialized in different content types).
- SEO Analyst AI (optimizes content).
- Client Liason AI (handles communication and revisions).
Initial SOPs: Task intake from client, content generation workflow, QA checklist, client feedback loop.
